# Flow Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 25, 2026_

## Summary
Flow does not collect, sell or share your personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking in the app.

## What Flow stores
Transactions, categories, notes, your budget, your name and your profile photo are stored only on your device. We never receive a copy of them.

## iCloud Sync
If you turn on iCloud Sync, your transactions are stored in your private iCloud database using Apple's CloudKit so they appear on your other devices. This data is protected by your Apple Account and is not accessible to us. You can turn sync off at any time in Settings > iCloud Sync.

## Assistant
The assistant runs entirely on your device. Its model is downloaded once from Hugging Face; that download, like any web request, reveals your IP address to the server. Your questions and transactions never leave your device.

## Face ID and passcode
App Lock uses Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode through iOS. Flow only learns whether the check succeeded; your biometric data never leaves the Secure Enclave.

## Notifications
Reminders are scheduled on your device. Flow does not use a server to send them, and you can turn them off at any time.

## Deleting your data
Deleting Flow removes all data stored on your device. To remove synced data, open the Settings app, tap your name, then iCloud > Manage Account Storage > Flow.

## Children
Flow is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page and in the app under Settings > About.

## Contact
If you have questions about your privacy, write to us at appsupport.flow@gmail.com.
